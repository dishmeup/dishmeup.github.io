# dishme

Save the dishes you love when you eat out, get a best-guess recipe to cook at home, and turn your favourites into one grocery list.

## How it works

1. **Add a dish:** take a photo of the plate, then type the menu line or photograph the menu. The menu is read on your phone (no upload).
2. **Best-guess recipe:** dishme matches the dish to about 50 built-in home recipes (carbonara, pad thai, fish tacos, ramen, shakshuka…) and adds any ingredients the menu mentions. With no close match, it builds a recipe from the dish type and the menu wording. Everything is editable.
3. **Groceries:** add dishes to the list, set servings, and get one combined list grouped by aisle. Copy or share it into Instacart, your store's app, or Notes.

Everything is stored on your device (IndexedDB). Back up and restore from the Dishes page. No accounts, servers, ads or tracking.

## Install on your phone

Open the site, then **Share → Add to Home Screen** (iPhone, Safari) or **⋮ → Install app** (Android, Chrome).
